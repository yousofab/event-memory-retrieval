# Phase 18 exact annotated-evidence feasibility and minimum-budget audit

## Why

Phase 17 matched the window opportunities and actual word costs, but its failures still mixed unreachable evidence, insufficient fixed windows and suboptimal window selection. A boundary policy cannot repair all three under the same constraints. This cycle measures exactly how much complete annotated coverage is still mathematically recoverable before investing in another memory architecture.

## Changes

- Adds a necessary-and-sufficient feasibility condition and constructive window witnesses inside the unchanged Phase 17 candidate space.
- Computes the exact minimum total budget for the frozen round-robin allocator, with proof and independent tiny-space enumeration.
- Audits 843 reused questions, 2,529 query–budget groups and all 55,638 historical conditions; no new answer model, GPU or paid API.
- Adds seven correctness tests, portable numeric validation, deterministic outputs, two figures, a 17-source primary literature review and Persian RTL B Nazanin reports.

## Findings

At 1,024 words, the writer's LoCoMo failures are 106 missing-session cases, 25 geometry-infeasible cases and 25 feasible selection misses. Exact feasible complete coverage is 468/599 versus the writer's recorded 443/599. Equal-conversation headroom is 4.19 percentage points, with a descriptive seven-conversation cluster interval of 2.96–5.13. This exceeds the predeclared two-point prioritization threshold, but approximately 84% of existing failures remain structurally unrecoverable under the frozen space.

QMSum permits complete coverage of all annotated relevant spans for 29/244 queries at 1,024 words, versus 23 recorded writer successes. This secondary joint-completeness outcome differs from Phase 17's mean partial-turn-recall outcome.

## Validation and limits

The full suite ran 59 tests: 58 passed and one historical Phase 14 test skipped because its pinned upstream data were absent. All seven Phase 18 tests ran. The portable validator checked 737 finite minimum-budget thresholds and 1,497 budget witnesses. Both numeric files reproduced byte-for-byte; the source-backed runner reproduced the parent geometry/fingerprints and re-scored all historical conditions. Previous result files are unchanged.

Local plan commit `daea6ca049478555650e6bcb3fb82449f5066d8c` preceded new scoring; implementation commit `c8bde1d0cfa2dd4d32d17236a1d705b3b31c8b56` preceded the first run. Registration was local, not independently publicly timestamped. The datasets and Phase 17 outcomes were already known. The bound is exact only for supplied annotations and the frozen legal windows; it is neither answer accuracy nor semantic sufficiency. Oracle witnesses use labels offline and are not a deployable retrieval method. The 240 seeded toy geometries are correctness tests, not a synthetic conversation benchmark.

The review has direct prior art for oracle diagnostics, sufficiency and event memory, including the Raw-RAG preprint of 22 October 2026. Two distinct MemTrace papers are disambiguated. The bounded contribution is this exact audit contract and minimum-budget formula; worldwide priority is not claimed.
