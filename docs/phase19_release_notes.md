# Session access and exact matched-edit audit

Completed Phase19 checkpoint, with an explicit negative primary finding and a
separately registered exploratory amendment. This is a research progress package,
not the final BSc thesis or a new answer-generating memory service.

Academic identity: Islamic Azad University, Karaj Branch; BSc Computer Engineering
project course. The researcher-supplied university logo is included on the Persian
report cover, alongside university supervision and FARDIAT educational thanks.

## Findings

At1,024 source words, reserving4 sessions is nearly a no-op and does not meet the
registered2-point advancement rule. Reserving8 sessions increases complete gold-
session access493→507 on LoCoMo and49→59 on EverMemBench, but lowers complete LoCoMo
evidence443→401 and external complete-reference-message recall19.53%→15.65%.
The latter percentages are equal-history means of different registered outcomes.

After the weak shuffled-label manipulation was exposed, an exploratory protocol
matched anchor-edit counts and derived an exact combinatorial access expectation.
Reserve8 improves access over that expectation but underperforms10 matched random
controls on coverage by4.01 and1.42 percentage points, respectively.
